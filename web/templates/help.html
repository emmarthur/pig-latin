How it works

Per word

  • Leading punctuation (like quotes or parentheses) stays at the front.
  • Trailing punctuation (commas, periods, etc.) stays at the end.
  • If the word starts with a consonant, those letters move to the end and ay is added.
  • If it starts with a vowel (including y), yay is added to the end.
  • ALL CAPS and Title Case are preserved on the translated word.

Examples

hello → ellohay  ·  eat → eatyay  ·  Hello, → Ellohay,
